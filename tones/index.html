The four shapes to look for

Recent syllables (example)

  • Height is pitch relative to your own voice: 1 is the bottom of your range, 5 is the top. The page learns your range as you talk.
  • The line is measured. The tone label on each syllable is a guess from the shape of the line.
  • Pitch is tracked with the raw autocorrelation method from Praat (Boersma 1993), using Praat's standard thresholds.
  • Sensitivity applies to the live microphone. Lower it if lines appear when nobody is speaking; raise it if a quiet voice is missed.
  • Leave a short pause between syllables to get one label per syllable. Run-together speech shows as one long line.
